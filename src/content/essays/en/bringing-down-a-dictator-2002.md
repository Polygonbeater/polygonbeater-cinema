Narrated by Martin Sheen, this award-winning documentary chronicles the meteoric rise of Otpor! (Resistance!), the Serbian student movement that toppled Slobodan Milošević in 2000. It is a masterclass in how a decentralized group of youth can outmaneuver a brutal state apparatus using disciplined strategy and sophisticated psychological marketing.

### Dismantling Fear with Strategic Humor

Otpor! understood that fear is the fuel of autocracy. To starve the regime, they used ridicule:
* **The "Arrested" Barrel:** Activists placed a barrel with Milošević’s face in a busy Belgrade street, inviting passers-by to hit it. When the police eventually "arrested" the inanimate object, the scene was broadcast worldwide, making the regime look absurd and shattering its aura of invincibility.

### Eroding the Pillars of Power

Otpor! systematically targeted the social and structural foundations of the regime:
* **The "Grandmother Strategy":** By deliberately getting arrested, students radicalized their own families. When "patriotic" parents and grandparents saw their children being beaten, they shifted from neutrality to active opposition.
* **Humanizing the "Enforcer" Pillar:** Activists spent months building bridges with individual police officers and soldiers, treating them as fellow citizens rather than enemies, which ultimately hollowed out the regime's enforcement wing.

### Decentralized Logistics and Message Discipline

Unlike traditional political parties, Otpor! was built to survive decapitation through cellular autonomy and the psychologically powerful **"Gotov Je!" (He is Finished!)** campaign.

### The Global Export: The Birth of CANVAS

The veterans of Otpor! founded CANVAS (Centre for Applied Nonviolent Action and Strategies), which has since trained activists in over 50 countries, serving as the strategic architect for subsequent global resistance movements.

### Conclusion: Otpor!’s Three Strategic Pillars

Unity, planning, and strict nonviolent discipline formed the triad of their revolutionary success.
