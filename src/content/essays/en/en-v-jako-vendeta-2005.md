The present study deconstructs the dystopian film *V for Vendetta* not as an action thriller, but as an instructional model for information and symbolic guerrilla warfare against undemocratic and populist governments. The text strictly separates the original intent of the creators (a comic book polemic on anarchy versus fascism) from the applied analysis (the use of anonymity, hijacking the state narrative). The methodological framework integrates the concept of the „Spiral of Silence“ (E. Noelle-Neumann) and the theory of „The Spectacle“ (G. Debord). The final phase transforms the theoretical framework into applied practice, defining tactics such as culture jamming and weaponized derision, which disrupt the monopoly of power without the need for physical confrontation.

### I. Author's Intent: The Idea as an Indestructible Construct
The film (based on the graphic novel by Alan Moore) was conceptualized as a warning against the apathy of a society that voluntarily surrenders its freedom in exchange for protection from a fabricated danger.

* **Artistic Vision:** The creators' intent was to demonstrate that in the face of absolute control, the individual fails if they fight solely as a physical entity. A physical entity can be arrested, discredited, or killed.
* **The Catalyst of Resistance:** The protagonist „V“ hides his face behind a Guy Fawkes mask not to protect himself, but to shed his personal identity and become a symbol. From the author's perspective, the primary message is the premise that ideas, unlike people, are bulletproof. An authoritarian apparatus can destroy a human being, but it cannot destroy a symbol that is adopted and replicated by thousands of other citizens.

### II. Analytical Interpretation: Breaking the „Spiral of Silence“
When applying political psychology to the plot, the targeted destruction of the sociological phenomenon known as the Spiral of Silence is identified.

* **Theoretical Framework:** Sociologist Elisabeth Noelle-Neumann defined that humans possess an instinctive evolutionary fear of isolation. If they believe their opinion is in the minority, they prefer to remain silent. Authoritarian governments and populists, by dominating the media space, create an artificial illusion of absolute consensus. This forces the opposition into silence, even if this opposition constitutes the factual majority.
* **Analytical Mechanism in the Narrative:** The government's monopoly on truth is shattered in the film when the protagonist „hijacks“ the national television broadcast. From a cognitive standpoint, it is not primarily important what exactly he says, but the very fact that he disrupted the monopoly of power. This sends a signal to all isolated citizens. The spiral of silence is broken, citizens lose their fear of isolation, and the psychological foundation of the system collapses before the physical one does.

### III. The Context of Civil Guerrilla: Semiotic Subversion
For the purposes of non-violent resistance, it is necessary to filter out physical aggression and focus on the concept of Semiotic Subversion (appropriating and twisting the symbols of power).

* **The Mechanism of The Spectacle:** According to philosopher Guy Debord, modern power operates through „The Spectacle“ (ubiquitous images, slogans, logos), which replaces authentic reality.
* **Application to the Narrative:** The protagonist does not destroy this spectacle exclusively with brute force; he rewrites it. Spray-painting the „V“ symbol on government posters does not change the physical nature of the paper, but it changes the meaning in the minds of the viewers. Government propaganda paradoxically becomes a canvas for a message of resistance and a tool for its own deconstruction.

### IV. Applied Practice: Information Guerrilla in Zone Zero
Based on the analysis, a model of asymmetric guerrilla warfare operating exclusively in the information and cultural space is defined. The goal is to disrupt the regime's illusion of untouchability and exhaust its resources while minimizing legal and physical risk to the actor.

* **Culture Jamming (Subvertising):** The tactic of visual recontextualization of public space.
  * *Execution:* The subject does not utilize their own limited resources to spread messages, but rather parasitizes the billion-dollar budgets of the system itself. Using marginally invasive interventions (stickers adding missing facts, QR codes leading to independent sources), they alter official posters and billboards. The original message of power is thus subverted, becoming a carrier of mockery directed at its own creator.
* **Weaponized Derision:** Undemocratic and populist systems are fundamentally built on threat and forced reverence for the leader. Fear is difficult to overcome, but mockery is cognitively stronger than fear.
  * *Execution:* The manipulator must never be taken seriously. The civil guerrilla creates absurd memes, parody accounts, and sarcastic caricatures. Laughter biologically lowers societal cortisol (stress) levels. Once the population begins laughing at the leader en masse, their aura of the omnipotent protector vanishes irreversibly. The apparatus cannot combat a joke with repression without appearing even more absurd.
* **Algorithmic Hijacking:** An asymmetric attack on the digital communication infrastructure without utilizing hacking.
  * *Execution:* Subjects in an organized manner utilize official government or populist hashtags and slogans, but attach content to them that refutes the official narrative. If the apparatus launches a PR campaign, the guerrilla floods this digital space with real data and evidence of failure. The promotional algorithm of the networks thus paradoxically distributes dissentient content.
* **Decentralized Anonymity (The Mask Principle):** The elimination of central management makes it impossible for the system to target repression.
  * *Execution:* Civil guerrilla actions exhibit an „open-source“ character. A flyer design or concept is created and shared anonymously on the network. Replication and physical distribution are carried out by thousands of isolated individuals acting on their own volition. The regime possesses tools to destroy a specific person (a leader), but lacks the mechanisms to halt swarm intelligence acting autonomously based on a shared symbol.

### V. Conclusion: The Bulletproof Nature of the Swarm
The definitive deconstruction of asymmetric resistance demonstrates that the greatest vulnerability of any manipulative system is its absolute dependence on PR, narrative control, and feigned infallibility. If the civil guerrilla abandons the idea of symmetric physical confrontation and focuses exclusively on information contamination and the destruction of the story the system tells about itself, its pressure becomes unstoppable. Malicious laughter, a recontextualized poster, and weaponized satire can dismantle authority more effectively than any weapon.

**Glossary of Technical Terms:**
* **Spiral of Silence:** A sociological theory (E. Noelle-Neumann) describing the tendency of people to remain silent and not express their opinions if they believe it is a minority view in society. This leads to the false impression that the dominant opinion has absolute support.
* **The Spectacle:** A philosophical concept (G. Debord) defining a state where authentic reality has been replaced by mere representations (images, slogans, the marketing of power).
* **Culture Jamming (Subvertising):** A form of guerrilla communication that disrupts dominant media culture and propaganda, typically by altering a logo or slogan to reveal the true nature of the message.
* **Semiotic Subversion (Détournement):** The process of taking an existing cultural symbol of power and deliberately twisting its meaning so that the symbol attacks its own creator.
* **Swarm Intelligence:** Decentralized, self-organizing behavior of a large number of individuals who act without central direction or a leader, yet together form a highly effective force.
