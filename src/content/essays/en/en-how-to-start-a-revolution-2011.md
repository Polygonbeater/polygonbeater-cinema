Directed by Ruaridh Arrow, this BAFTA-winning documentary profiles Dr. Gene Sharp, a scholar whose work shifted the world’s understanding of revolution. Sharp’s core insight is that a dictatorship is not a solid, immovable object, but a fragile structure held upright by the voluntary and withdrawable cooperation of the people.

### The Logic of the Pillars

Sharp’s strategy begins with identifying the "Pillars of Support." Rulers require an army, bureaucracy, media, and business elite to govern. When a movement targets these pillars, they attack the foundations keeping the dictator in place, aiming for "total social friction."

### The Serbian Masterclass and Political Jiu-Jitsu

The documentary explores how Otpor! in Serbia weaponized humor and professional branding against Slobodan Milošević. Furthermore, Sharp explains Political Jiu-Jitsu: using the regime’s own brutal force against peaceful protesters to create a "backfire" effect that shatters state legitimacy.

### Modernization and Digital Resistance

In the digital era, traditional pillars are increasingly replaced by algorithms, surveillance systems, and financial controls. Modern movements utilize decentralized finance (DeFi), encryption (E2EE), and decentralized protocols to build untouchable parallel institutions.

### Strategy over Spontaneity

Sharp emphasizes the "Two-Phase" rule: Phase 1 removes the dictator, while Phase 2 must instantly fill the vacuum with robust democratic structures to prevent a new autocrat from taking over.
