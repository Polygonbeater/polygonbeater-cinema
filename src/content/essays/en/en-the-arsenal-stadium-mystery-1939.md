> *“A visionary blend of sports action and detective mystery, filmed on the brink of World War II.”*

Directed by Thorold Dickinson and adapted from Leonard Gribble’s novel, *The Arsenal Stadium Mystery* (1939) stands as a landmark in sports cinema, uniquely placing football at the core of its narrative puzzle.

## Autenticity and Pre-War Snapshot

The plot begins when an amateur player collapses and dies from digitalis poisoning during a charity match at Highbury against the real Arsenal F.C. Eccentric Scotland Yard Inspector Anthony Slade (Leslie Banks) uncovers a web of jealousy and blackmail:

* **Unprecedented Authenticity:** Dickinson seamlessly intercut staged drama with genuine footage from an actual league match played on May 6, 1939—Arsenal's final game before the suspension of the league due to war.
* **Documentary-Style Casting:** Real Arsenal players and staff, including legendary manager George Allison, appear as themselves, elevating the film into an invaluable historical artifact.

> *„Beyond a gripping murder mystery, the film offers a poignant, nostalgic glimpse into British society during the final moments of peace.“*
